Astro Chain - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Astro Chain (the "Game"), made by Plum Rocket Games ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: the Game does not collect, store on our side, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not use the internet.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of game data only on your own device: scores, unlocked astronauts and their levels, in-game currency, items, which tips you have already seen, and settings such as sound, screen shake and vibration. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, to give short vibrations during play (you can turn this off in the Game's settings). It does not request any other permissions.

Third parties

The Game does not include advertising, analytics or social SDKs, and we do not sell or share any data with anyone. The Game is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Because the Game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the Game ever starts handling information differently (for example by adding ads or online features), we will update this page and change the date above before that version is released.
