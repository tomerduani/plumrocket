Astro Chain - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Astro Chain (the "Game"), made by Plum Rocket Games ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: we do not collect any personal information ourselves. The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device information as described below.

Information we collect

None. The Game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run our own analytics or servers. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts or photos.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of game data only on your own device: scores, unlocked astronauts and their levels, in-game currency, items, which tips you have already seen, and settings such as sound, screen shake and vibration. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads you choose to watch (for example to continue a game), and occasionally a full-screen ad appears between games.

To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized. Google handles this data under its own policies - see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown (using Google's consent tool), and you can choose non-personalized ads. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads).

Permissions

The Game uses internet access (to load ads), the advertising ID permission (used by AdMob) and vibration (short vibrations during play, which you can turn off in the Game's settings). It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share any data with anyone ourselves. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob, and Google Play, which distributes the Game).

Changes to this policy

If the Game starts handling information differently, we will update this page and change the date above before that version is released.
